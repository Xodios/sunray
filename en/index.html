Raising your children on your own?

SunRay welcomes single mothers and their children in Nice and the surrounding area, especially when life feels harder to bear.

Perhaps you are going through:

  • isolation
  • a separation
  • a bereavement
  • social or family difficulties
  • a difficult time in your life

Here, we listen to you, you meet other mums, and you get support to move forward at your own pace.

What we offer

Friendly get-togethers, workshops and one-to-one support to take care of yourself, connect with others and build your future.

Meet-ups for single mothers

Friendly get-togethers to talk, share your experiences and build connections in a caring environment.

French language workshops and the values of the French Republic

To improve your communication, become more independent in daily life and better understand French society and its values.

Makeovers and well-being

Workshops led by the women of the group, who share their talents (hairdressing, make-up, face and body care, style tips…) to take care of yourself, feel valued and regain self-confidence.

Activities for you and your children

Friendly moments and activities designed for you and your children (creative workshops, outings, leisure…) to share quality time, strengthen your bond and help your children thrive.

Your personal plan

One-to-one support to set your goals and move towards a future that suits you: work, training, housing, family life, independence.

Referrals to partner organisations

We put you in touch with the right organisations for your needs: rights, housing, health, work, training, childcare…

And also: discussion groups and speaking workshops, to find the courage to speak up and make your voice heard.

How does it work?

  1. 1

    Get in touch

    By phone or by email, whichever you prefer.

  2. 2

    We get to know you

    We take the time to listen: your situation, your needs, your wishes.

  3. 3

    Move forward at your own pace

    Meet-ups, workshops, activities with your children, one-to-one support: you choose what suits you. And when we don’t have the answer, we point you to the right organisation.

SunRay is a bridge

We can’t do everything ourselves, but we know where to send you. When we can help you, we do; when we can’t, we connect you with the right people and organisations.

Based in Nice, SunRay supports single mothers and their children and promotes their independence, well-being and fulfilment. Our work rests on four values.

Confidence
Building confidence and self-esteem, and recognising each woman’s skills and know-how.
Solidarity
Breaking isolation, building connections and encouraging mutual support between single mothers.
Independence
Becoming more independent and taking an active part in your children’s upbringing.
Success
Building your own plans (work, training, housing, family life) and playing a full part in society.

Get involved

Are you a local organisation (employment, housing, health, training, childcare, access to rights), a professional, a volunteer or a donor? Let’s work together to support and guide single mothers and their children.
